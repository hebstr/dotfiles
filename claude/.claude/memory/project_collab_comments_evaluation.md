`MikeLydeamore/collab-comments` (Quarto filter) plus `MichaelLydeamore.collab-comments` (Positron, OpenVSX 1.1.0) was evaluated on 2026-10-23 for `eds-prise`: carrying the 24 comment points of the reviewed manuscript, `docs/csi/review/2026-09-29_prise_csi_review.docx` since the move of 2026-10-24 and `docs/review/2026-09-29_prise_review-manuscrit.docx` in every dated record below, back into the docx render of `docs/csi/csi.qmd`, so the reviewer receives an updated Word in which her remarks stay answerable.

**Adopted, both halves.** Full reading, arithmetic, revert path and trial gate in `~/dotfiles/_meta/notes/collab-comments-reco.md`. Four facts worth having before touching it:

- The decision rests on durability, not on coverage: a `.comments/collab-comments.json` entry is re-applied at every render, where a manual edit of a rendered Word dies at the next one. **Coverage measured on 2026-10-24: 6 of the 24 points carry under upstream, 13 under the fork, and 23 once ten entries are written by hand**, the import having written 13 entries of which upstream skips 7 and the fork none; the twenty-fourth aimed at headings since removed, so it is closed as moot rather than anchored. Three of the ten share one table caption, split into disjoint spans, which is what a comment aimed at a table's body or at the figure after it can be given; another took the document's first heading, a comment on the whole plan having no block of its own, the subtitle being metadata no store entry reaches. The reading's 13/10/1 arithmetic described the import alone.
- **What kills an anchor under the fork is the selection, not the block.** The three patch stairs of 2026-10-24 reach a paragraph inside a list, a Div or a table cell, recurse into a bold run, a link or a span, and treat a citation, a code span, a formula, an image, a footnote and raw markup as opaque runs a selection can sit beside. What is left: a selection occurring more than once in the rendered document, one crossing a source line, one whose text has been rewritten since the import, and one whose boundary falls inside an opaque run. Upstream, by contrast, refuses the whole block on a citation anywhere in it, any list item and a wholly bold paragraph, which is the behaviour to expect from an unforked install.
- **An included fragment is outside the export altogether, measured on 2026-10-28**, which is a file boundary rather than a selection: `exported_comments()` keeps an entry only when its `filePath` equals the rendered document's own path relative to the root carrying `.comments/`, and `source_lines()` reads that document's source alone and follows no `{{< include >}}`. So a comment anchored in the fragment then under measure, `docs/snds/snds-plan-chunk.qmd`, which the user deleted on 2026-10-29 in reversing the split, reached neither the docx of `docs/csi/csi.qmd`, which included it as an annex, nor that of `docs/snds/snds-plan.qmd`, which declared no `collab-comments:` block until 2026-10-29; and it is silent, the entry never being a candidate, so no `[collab-comments]` line reaches stderr and a gate built on that silence stays green. Forcing the entry's `filePath` to the includer does not help either, and not for want of the literal: the stored range is then stale and the fallback of `selected_source_text()` demands the literal occur exactly once in the includer's source, which measured on 2026-10-28 no anchorable line of the fragment does, 31 of its 181 non-empty lines appearing verbatim in `csi.qmd` and only one of them anchorable, the heading `## Sources de données`, which `csi.qmd` carries twice. What stays commentable of such an annex is the heading that lives in the includer. Deferred the same day in `~/Documents/packages/quarto-hebstr-doc/.claude/DEFERRED.md`, entry of 2026-10-28, which carries the minimal shape of the fix; nothing consumes it yet.
- **Dropping the `filters:` line costs two source lines, and a stored range is an absolute line number.** Removing it shifts every anchor and the filter never reads `documentHash`, so before the fallback of 1.7.2 the thirteen all reported `stored selection is stale or not literal source text` rather than an off-by-two (measured 2026-10-24: 13 of 13 resolve at the current offset, 0 of 13 at minus two); since it, a shifted range is absorbed wherever the stored `selectedText` occurs exactly once in the source and reported as a `drifted Word comment`, and only a rewritten or repeated selection still costs the balloon. Let the editor extension re-anchor on that edit, or shift the stored line numbers with it. The first is what fired on 2026-10-24: the lines were removed with `csi.qmd` open in Positron, the extension remapped all thirteen ranges and rewrote `documentHash`, and the render that follows carries 13 `w:comment` and no skip. `documentHash` is `sha256` of the document text, so the hand shift stays computable for an edit made with no editor on the file. **That edit landed on 2026-10-26 and it is silent**: `b2eaa4d` then `7d97a4d` moved the `setup` chunk by a net of one line, all eighteen entries died at once, the render exited 0, and the loss surfaced only at the CHU delivery render weeks later; a uniform shift of minus one restored eighteen of eighteen, verified at 18 `w:comment`. The editor half does not cover this case: its re-anchoring runs on `onDidChangeTextDocument` alone, and the restore pass that re-anchors every comment at load mutates memory and persists nothing (read in the bundle on 2026-10-26).
- The editor half is installed for its re-anchoring (`onDidChangeTextDocument` rewrites every stored range as the source moves), not for its import. Under a document still being rewritten by hand, a repaired store rots silently otherwise. **It is not a guarantee, measured on 2026-10-24**: having remapped thirteen anchors correctly when the `filters:` lines went, it later wrote on six of twenty entries a `trackedChanges` whose `previousText` was the stored selection and whose `newText` was the whole document, with a range covering the document, taking the store from 20 KB to 1.33 MB; the fourteen others were untouched and the document had been reformatted by panache in the same minute, no line moving. Nothing in the render catches it and the balloon would carry the whole document, so a store is validated against the source before any render that ships and before any commit, and the extension is closed on the document once the store is final.
- **It does not carry Word tracked changes, measured on the 1.1.0 bundle and on the fork on 2026-10-24, so it never replaces `/review-prose`.** The store's `trackedChanges` field is unrelated to `w:ins` / `w:del`: the editor half records how the *commented span* was rewritten in the `.qmd` (`previousText` / `newText`, author, timestamp) and the filter prints it as a `—— Tracked change · author · date ——` block inside the balloon text. The importer runs `quarto pandoc <docx> --track-changes=all --to=json` but reads only `comment-start` / `comment-end` spans, never `insertion` / `deletion`, and it offers no import command for them; the extension's only tracked-change UI is the `Merge Tracked Changes` thread action (`merge-tracked-changes`, a webview action and not a registered command), which folds the `.qmd` edit records. The export emits comments only. **The user judged those records pure noise on 2026-10-28 and had them deleted**: 19 of them across 8 of the 19 `eds-prise` entries, every one a keystroke-level capture of a typo, a parenthesis or an agreement, emptied to `trackedChanges: []`, which is the form the bundle itself normalises to (`trackedChanges ?? []` at load, the schema check accepting the key absent). **They refill unconditionally**: recording runs on `onDidChangeTextDocument` with no setting to disable it and no command to clear one, `merge-tracked-changes` keeping one record per span, so emptying them is a sweep before a delivery render rather than a one-off. The directions are opposite too: `/review-prose` goes docx → source, collab-comments goes store → docx.
- A render that loses comments **exits 0**; the only trace is `[collab-comments] ...: skipped Word comment <id>: <reason>` on stderr. Read it at every delivery render.
- The export is stricter than the import: the import disambiguates several occurrences by stored context, the export demands exactly one occurrence in the rendered document.

**Word accepts the package, measured at the gate**: Word 16.0.20430 on `ju-TP2` opens it with no repair prompt, updates the table-of-contents field and anchors every comment on its exact stored span, mid-word starts included. Pandoc writes no `w:initials`, so a re-emitted balloon reads `Commented [1]:` where one the reviewer wrote reads `Commented [GV1]:`; her name stays in `w:author`. Re-run on the fork's own output the same day: the thirteen balloons are numbered 1 to 13 with no gap and each sits on its exact span, the seven new ones landing beside a citation, inside two bullet items, inside a bullet item that is wholly bold, inside an ordered-list continuation and on a top-level wholly bold paragraph. The kill condition is spent for every shape the fork reaches. Re-run again on the twenty-entry store the same day: twenty balloons, numbered 1 to 20 with no gap over 39 pages, **a float caption among them**, anchored on the caption text with Quarto's `Table N.` prefix outside the highlight, which is the one shape a hand-written entry adds.

Revisit trigger, **and the first one fired on 2026-10-26, from the fork rather than from upstream**: `selected_source_text()` keeps the stored range as a fast path and falls back to `selectedText` when it occurs exactly once in the source, so the stored range becomes a diagnostic. Decided and implemented that day, cut into 1.7.2, designed in `~/Documents/packages/quarto-hebstr-doc/.claude/DESIGN-COLLAB-COMMENTS.md`, section "A stale stored range costs the comment no longer", and pinned by `test_a_range_spanning_source_lines_resolves_on_its_unique_literal` with its two siblings; a drift resolved that way is named on stderr as a `drifted Word comment` rather than as a skip. The stored `contextBefore` / `contextAfter` turn out not to be needed for it: that function returns `comment.selectedText` on every success and its one consumer is the search in the rendered document, so the source-side check validates an anchor rather than producing one, and `occurrences == 1` over the rendered blocks is what places the balloon. The threading trigger is spent too, and the fork answered it rather than upstream: since 2026-10-27 every reply is a Word comment of its own anchored on its parent's span, and hebstr-doc ships `_extensions/hebstr-doc/scripts/thread-docx-comments.py`, which a consuming project wires as a `post-render` entry to write `word/commentsExtended.xml` after the zip is closed; without that entry the replies arrive as separate comments in chronological order. **A real Word read a threaded package on 2026-10-28**, Word 16.0.20527 on the 19-comment store of `eds-prise`, through a hand-synced install of the unreleased tree and a temporary `post-render` list of two: what settles it is that Word numbers the balloons itself, nineteen roots 1 to 19 with no gap and each reply under its parent's number suffixed `R`, where a package it refused to thread would have numbered the three replies of comment 9 as 10, 11 and 12. The tracked changes stay in the opening comment's body, so one body holding six versions of a clause costs one balloon and not six.
**The `w:comment` count a gate compares against is 31 and not 19, measured on 2026-10-28**: the 19 store entries carry 12 nested `replies` across 10 of them, each emitted as a `w:comment` of its own, so a gate that expects the store's entry count reads a regression where there is none. The `post-render` step prints the arithmetic itself, `[thread-docx-comments] <file>: 31 comments, 12 threaded under 10`, and it goes to **stdout** where the filter's own `[collab-comments]` lines go to stderr, so a render captured with `2>` alone loses it. The trigger that would have overturned the adoption, a real Word refusing the package, is spent.

**Why**: two earlier verdicts exist and neither answers this question, so a pass unaware of them would redo both. **How to apply**: a delivery render is read for every `[collab-comments]` line rather than for the `skipped Word comment` ones alone, the adversarial walkthrough of 2026-10-26 having found that a store the schema check refuses loses the whole export at once, carries no comment id and used to say nothing at all: the three store refusals are named since then (`comment store is not valid JSON`, `comment store holds no comment collection`, `comment entry N does not match the comment schema`), as is a `collab-comments:` value written as a scalar instead of a block, which used to kill the render outright; both fixes shipped in hebstr-doc 1.7.4 on 2026-10-26 and the install here took them the same day, `version: 1.7.4` and `collab-comments.lua` byte-identical to that tree. The fallback of 1.7.2 took the uniform shift out of the skip count while leaving a rewritten or repeated selection in it; what no mechanism catches any more is the editor half's corruption, the range check having caught it by accident, so a store is still validated against the source before a render that ships. Do not re-evaluate the tool, and never re-propose the hand-written OOXML injection script, measured and set aside on 2026-10-23. A straight-apostrophe linter was considered and rejected: the filter's own stderr reports every broken anchor completely, where a linter would be a proxy with false positives on every apostrophe no comment anchors to. **The apostrophe itself is taken out of the failure set on 2026-10-26, decided and delivered the same day in hebstr-doc 1.7.3**: the comparator normalises it on both sides, the source side counting and comparing normalised while the rendered side picks the needle between the stored literal and its twin, so neither form costs an anchor and a consumer's source form becomes free; `eds-prise` keeps the typographic form and its `smart = false` all the same, and the linter stays rejected. Reasoning, the keyboard and source-discipline routes rejected with it and the measurements in `~/Documents/packages/quarto-hebstr-doc/.claude/DESIGN-COLLAB-COMMENTS.md`, section "The apostrophe form of the source costs the comment nothing". **The fork landed on 2026-10-24 and is not reopened**: the docx branch alone is forked and patched into `quarto-hebstr-doc`, at `_extensions/hebstr-doc/filters/collab-comments.lua` wired `at: post-quarto` under `docx:`, the cost of owning a patch against a one-author upstream accepted along with the loss of the `quarto update collab-comments` route, and the editor half stays upstream unmodified, a patch to it recovering only 6 of the 11 never-imported points where a hand-written store entry recovers each of them. The fork's own decisions, the three stairs and the two defects they uncovered are in `~/Documents/packages/quarto-hebstr-doc/.claude/DESIGN-COLLAB-COMMENTS.md`; the project half, store included, is in `eds-prise/.claude/DESIGN-INDEX.md`.
**The `/review-comments` register is never rebuilt from the store, rejected on 2026-10-24**: a stored span is chosen for where a balloon can land, not for what the comment is about, measured on four of the 24 points (ranks 22, 23 and 24 carry three contiguous mid-word fragments of one table caption, rank 1 a heading for a comment on the whole Methods plan), so a register built on it records a false localisation. The premise of a complete store worsens this rather than saving it, a store being completed *by* relocating spans; the two reply threads were hand-written from the register, the import filing none; and no project but `eds-prise` holds a store, so a global skill keeps its docx path. What stays open is the store as a side-input, for the day a second reviewed docx lands on a document whose store exists: it is then the only key telling a re-emitted point from a new one. Reasoning in the reco note, section "Rejected on 2026-10-24: the register is never rebuilt from the store".

**A second use arrived on 2026-10-29 and it is not review: the store carries a document's own references.** The user decided that the eight parenthetical links and their verbatim quotes of the « Sources de données » section of `docs/snds/snds-plan.qmd` leave the prose, which keeps its argument alone, and become eight hand-written store entries anchored on a unique fragment each, so the store holds 28 entries over two documents and a `w:comment` count is per document, 8 on the plan against 32 on `csi.qmd`. Three facts the build settled. The entry body is literal text, `literal_inlines()` emitting `Str`, `Space` and `LineBreak` only, so a markdown link in a comment would print as its own source and a bare URL is the form that reads: label, URL, then the quotes, one per line. `range.start.line` is 0-based and `character` a 0-based offset into that line, and `documentHash` is the sha256 of the document's exact bytes, both verified against the existing `csi.qmd` entries before writing any. The entries were assembled by a script reading the links and quotes out of the pre-edit blob through `git show`, never retyped, which is also how an external URL reaches a file without being re-verified. What the route costs, stated to the user and accepted: the HTML carries no reference at all, the fork having dropped the HTML export, and the anchors sit on prose the user may still rewrite, where the unique-literal fallback absorbs a line shift but not a reformulation. The footnote alternative was measured the same day and turned down by the user; its one durable by-product is a panache defect recorded in [[project_qmd_format_hook]].
