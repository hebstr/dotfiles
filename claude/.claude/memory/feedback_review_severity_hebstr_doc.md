Package-specific review dismissals for the `quarto-hebstr-doc` Quarto extension at `~/Documents/packages/quarto-hebstr-doc`, supplementing [[feedback_review_severity_personal]]. Distinct from [[feedback_review_severity_hebstr]] (the R package). See also [[reference_quarto_lua_shortcodes]], and `rules/lua.md` for the Lua gate.

**Why:** successive walkthroughs of `filters/filetree.lua` and `tests/test_filetree.lua` produced recurring idiom, performance and duplication findings that a future reviewer will re-flag; each was assessed and dismissed on the merits.

**How to apply (do NOT re-flag these):**
- **The 7-line `kw` helper duplicated between `filetree.lua` and `add-code-files.lua` is deliberate, not a refactor target.** Each shortcode is an autonomous entry point (CLAUDE.md calls them "autonomous"); factoring `kw` out costs a shared module plus a `package.path` derived from `PANDOC_SCRIPT_FILE` in each consumer, reopening the install-depth hazard CLAUDE.md already documents twice (filetree icons, and the Typst `code.tmTheme`, which went with the Typst format dropped on 2026-09-29 by `34e7003`, leaving the icons as the live example). ~20 lines of indirection + a new failure mode to save 7 stable lines. Rejected; the copies must stay byte-identical, so keep `kw` aligned across both.
- **The hardcoded `/` path separator is a deliberate single internal convention, not a `pandoc.path.join` omission.** `relpath` is the annotation-key AND display string; the sidecar is authored with `/` cross-platform, so `pandoc.path.join` (which emits `\` on Windows) would break key matching. `/` also works for filesystem access on all three OSes. Splitting the convention only for the FS-path joins would leave `relpath` on `/` two lines away, more confusing than uniform `/`. NOTED, no change.
- **No `list_directory` cache.** Measured 7 redundant listings of 66 on a depth-3 walk (each of 7 dirs listed twice: once to type via `classify`, once to read). Once-per-render, depth-bounded, OS dentry-cached; a cache is dead metadata by the premise test. NOTED.
- **The `warn` guard (`quarto and quarto.log...` + stderr fallback) vs the unguarded `quarto.doc.is_format` is intentional asymmetry.** `warn` is standalone-safe for helper testing; `is_format` has no non-quarto behavior and a nil-`quarto` crash there is already a clear error. The `project_dir = quarto and quarto.project...` guard is semantic (project legitimately absent in single-file render), a third distinct case, not part of the asymmetry. NOTED.
- **The local `kwargs()` helper of `tests/test_filetree.lua` mutating the table it is handed is not a defect.** It writes a default `annotations` into the caller's literal, and all 16 call sites pass a fresh literal (grepped 2026-09-29). The trap a reviewer names, a shared options table, is neutralised by the defect itself: the write is guarded by `== nil`, so a reused table gets the same value on the second call and the outcome is identical. A defensive copy has nothing to consume it. REJECTED 2026-09-29.
- **A zero-occurrence coercion edge case in the sidecar reader is NOTED, not fixed, unless it contradicts YAML itself.** A fractional `depth` is the worked example: measured 2026-09-29, the static render takes its ceiling (`2.1` already shows three levels) and the dynamic opening its floor, the two never disagreeing inside one mode, and no sidecar in use holds such a value. What earned a fix in that same pass, by contrast, was every case where the hand-rolled reader disagreed with a real YAML parser on the same file, since `read_sidecar` runs `pandoc.read` over it too: a trailing comment on an `exclude` item, a flow-mapping profile, a quoted profile name.
- **The seven noted edge cases of `filters/docx-caption.lua` are decided, not missed.** Walkthrough of 2026-10-25 on that filter: eight findings, one real and fixed (a layout row of several cells now loses its column alignment, Pandoc writing it as a direct `w:jc` that outranks `Table Caption`, so one float's two sub-captions read differently aligned), seven NOTED on a measurement each: a Markdown hard break left as `w:br` (nine captions carry a break across `~/Documents` and `~/admin`, all raw `<br>`), `restyle` returning at the first caption of a block list (no Quarto shape puts two in one list, two panel floats in one document included), `top = i < #blocks`, `single_cell` ignoring `col_span` (zero `w:gridSpan` in either rendered probe), an empty caption title orphaning its subtitle (no `str_fig("")` among fourteen call sites), a bottom table caption not centred as HTML centres it (position is the DOCX rule), and `is_caption` matching a substring. Measurements in the "What the walkthrough of 2026-10-25 kept and what it left" section of the project's `.claude/DOCX-GT-WIDTH.md`; do not re-flag them without a real incident.
- **The six noted edge cases of `filters/collab-comments.lua` are decided, not missed.** Walkthrough of 2026-10-26 on that filter and its suite: fourteen findings, eight accepted (two diagnostic silences closed, ten test cases added, two trivial simplifications) and six NOTED on a measurement each, the asymmetry of `rendered_needle` between a stored form and its apostrophe twin, the counting walk not reaching inline-only containers (a `title:`, a definition-list term, a `LineBlock` line, a short caption; zero line blocks and zero definition lists in `csi.qmd`), `utf8.codepoint` raising on invalid UTF-8 (pandoc refuses such a source before any filter runs), `comment.timestamp` reaching `w:date` validated as a string only (all seventeen store timestamps ISO), a `word_id` collision dropping both comments (~7e-8 on seventeen entries, no cheap fixture forces one), and the store walk climbing past `quarto.project.directory` (bounding it breaks a render outside a project). Measurements in the "What the adversarial walkthrough of 2026-10-26 kept and what it left" section of the project's `.claude/DESIGN-COLLAB-COMMENTS.md`; do not re-flag them without a real incident, and read that whole note before reviewing the fork, whose divergences from upstream are deliberate.
- **The ten noted edge cases of `_extensions/hebstr-doc/scripts/thread-docx-comments.py` are decided, not missed.** Walkthrough of 2026-10-27 on that script: seventeen findings, six accepted (the extended part rewritten over Word's own threading, `zlib.error` outside the caught tuple, a partial `.threading` zip left beside the deliverable, a first failure aborting the remaining outputs, an unreadable output listing raising a traceback, the lost `standalone="yes"`), one rejected (a paraId collision that cannot arise, `w15:paraIdParent` resolving only among the paragraphs of `comments.xml`) and ten noted on a measurement each, among them the `minidom` attribute-value normalisation on tab, LF and CR with zero occurrence over 100 store values, the file mode not carried by `replace`, the `roots`/`threaded` disagreement, and the schema-valid but unproduced `w:comment` with no `w:p`. The six are pinned by `tests/thread-docx.py`, twenty assertions over its own synthetic packages, each checked red against a mutated copy of the script first. Measurements in the "What the walkthrough of 2026-10-27 on the threading script kept and what it left" section of the project's `.claude/DESIGN-COLLAB-COMMENTS.md`; do not re-flag them without a real incident. Python is under the commit gate since that pass (`ruff-check`, `ruff-format` and a local `pyrefly -p default` in `prek.toml`), so a missing-Python-hook finding is closed rather than calibrated away.
- **Terse helper names (`kw`, `esc`, `opt`) are conventional Lua-filter idiom, not a naming defect.** `esc` = HTML escape, `opt` = option getter, `kw` = kwarg getter (and must stay aligned with `add-code-files.lua`). NOTED. The same holds for a module constant: `docx-layout-flatten.lua`'s `KEYS` sits beside the `STYLE`, `TYPE`, `LANG`, `PAGE_BREAK`, `CLOSING_PARAGRAPH` and `CAPTION_MARKER` of its siblings (measured 2026-10-25), and that file deals in no other family of keys, so a `LAYOUT_KEYS` rename is not owed. REJECTED 2026-10-25.
- **The decided items of `filters/docx-layout-flatten.lua` are in the "What the walkthrough of 2026-10-25 on this filter kept and what it left" section of the project's `.claude/DOCX-GT-WIDTH.md`.** Three noted on a measurement each (`layout-align` and `layout-valign` dropped where Quarto's `attr_has_layout_attributes` builds no table, `support.load_filter` defaulting to the first pass, the accessibility cost of the shapes the filter does not reach) and one rejected on a refuted premise. The confusion to expect from a reviewer is the authoring form: what bounds that filter is float promotion, so an unlabelled chunk and a labelled chunk with no subcaption are reached like a bare fenced row, while a fenced row carrying a float id and a labelled chunk with `fig-subcap` are not. A finding whose example is `csi.qmd` or `tests/docx-template-probe.qmd` is citing a float-promoted shape the filter never touches. The user declined the float handler that would close the gap on 2026-10-25, documenting the limit instead, so do not re-propose it without a real incident.
- **The four costs of `filters/docx-callout-flatten.lua` are the request, not defects of it.** Decided with the user in `/design` on 2026-10-29, implemented and released as v1.9.0 the same day; measurements, the five approved calls and the four rejected places in the project's `.claude/DESIGN-DOCX-CALLOUTS.md`. (a) The aside is lost: a bodyless callout becomes a lone bold paragraph that reads as a run-in subheading, sharpest in a run of them, measured under Word 16.0.20527 on 2026-10-29 with captures under `.claude/screenshots/callouts/`; ten of `eds-prise`'s thirteen callouts are in that shape, and the knob left is the spacing around the title paragraph, never the table back. (b) The title comes back as a `Para` of `Strong` rather than the heading it was authored as, `resolveHeadingCaption` having already dropped the level and the attributes by the time the `Callout` node exists, and a heading restored here would take a section number, a table of contents entry and, at level 1, a `pageBreakBefore` from `template.dotx`. (c) A callout carrying an identifier is exempted and keeps Quarto's table: flattening at `pre-quarto` means `crossref_callouts` never sees it, so a flattened one would render `?@nte-x`; the exemption is deliberate, not a missed branch. (d) `appearance`, `icon` and `collapse` are read by none of it, the `Callout` constructor consuming all three before any renderer sees the node. The weakest assumption, that Quarto keeps that node reachable from a user filter, is watched by `tests/docx-template.sh` and cannot be closed by review. No render of this repository exercises the filter, `example.qmd` declaring HTML alone, so the probe is the whole coverage by design: a test-coverage finding resting on the absent DOCX render of `example.qmd` is step 3 of the DOCX validation item, deferred by the user since 2026-09-15, not a gap.
- **The six noted edge cases of the DOCX assertion surface are decided, not missed.** Walkthrough of 2026-10-29 on `tests/docx-template.sh`, `scripts/check-docx.R` and `filters/docx-callout-flatten.lua` with its suite: 24 findings, 18 accepted (four silently-green holes closed, two misdirecting messages corrected, the 481-line assertion block extracted to `tests/docx-template-assert.py`, which put those assertions under the repository's Python hooks for the first time, the hooks themselves dating from 2026-10-27) and six noted on a measurement each, none rejected. (a) The identifier guard is wider than its own rationale and the exempted path delivers the number without the bookmark, both measured on quarto 1.10.19, but **zero of the 97 callout divs of `~/Documents` and `~/admin` carry an identifier**, so a narrower guard has nothing to consume it. (b) The discarded `callout.attr`: of the 21 callout divs carrying anything beyond their `.callout-*` class, every extra key is one of `appearance`, `collapse` and `icon`, which the constructor consumes before the node exists, so none is in the `Attr` the handler drops. (c) The bold check matching `w:b` anywhere in the title paragraph is loose, and the flatten cannot produce a partially bold title, the whole inlines list going into one `Strong`; the condition is bidirectional since that pass and measured clean (`True` on both titles, `False` on both bodies). (d) An explicit non-vacuity guard on the `w:tc` loop is already covered by the positional caption comparison and, since the same pass, by the exempted callout's own table. (e) The three unguarded `read_xml` of `check-docx.R`: every input is a Pandoc render, which always writes `settings.xml`, `styles.xml` and `fontTable.xml`, and the raw R error names the missing file. (f) `check_geometry` ignores `w:gutter`, measured 0 in the template, and the contract is shared with `hebstr::docx_page_width()`, so a one-sided fix would make the two disagree; it belongs to a pass that touches both repositories. One residual was declined inside an accepted finding rather than noted on its own: pinning the flattened title paragraph's own style would pin Pandoc's `FirstParagraph`/`BodyText` alternation rather than the flatten. Measurements in the project's `.claude/DESIGN-DOCX-CALLOUTS.md` and `.claude/DOCX-TEMPLATE.md`; do not re-flag without a real incident. Two remedies that pass's reviewer proposed were measured wrong and replaced: `setdiff(referenced, embedded)` on the media check flags `footer1.xml` on a sound render, where `referenced[!file.exists(referenced)]` does not, and adding a `@nte-x` reference to the probe would make the bookmark condition permanently red, the exempted callout taking no `w:bookmarkStart`.
- **The five noted edge cases of `filters/add-code-files.lua` are decided, not missed.** Walkthrough of 2026-10-29 on the `{{< script >}}` shortcode and its suite: 19 findings, 14 accepted (two defects reaching the deliverable, three diagnostic silences, the `lines=N` form, nine cases over the seven uncovered branches) and five noted, none rejected. (a) `dedent` strips spaces and never tabs, which is `README.md`'s own wording, a guessed tab width corrupting alignment. (b) The `R = "r"` row of `lang_from_ext` is redundant with the `ext:lower()` fallback and is kept, where `Rprofile = "r"` is live, `{{< script .Rprofile >}}` being called in three real projects (measured over `~/Documents` and `~/admin`). (c) `lang=""` cannot suppress highlighting, `kw` returning its default on an empty string, and the no-language form is reachable anyway through an extension absent from the table; nothing changed there because `kw` is the byte-identical copy of `filetree.lua`'s, so a warning would have to land in both for a form nobody writes. (d) `js_registered` is per-document state rather than a leak, measured on a two-document project render whose every output carries exactly one JS block, the first document calling the shortcode twice. (e) Security category, evaluated on its merits rather than calibrated away: the path is unconstrained where `filetree.lua` guards its `annotations`, and that asymmetry is deliberate, a path leaving the project being a legitimate target and the render already executing the document's chunks, so a lexical guard would cost the monorepo case and buy nothing a symlink could not walk around. One finding was downgraded from Required to NOTED on a defense both cross-model levels corroborated: an authoring mistake is silent outside HTML, and the output there is empty whether the call is right or wrong, so moving `error()` above the `html:js` guard would abort a Word render over a shortcode that contributes nothing to Word and would still miss a path typo. Measurements in the project's `.claude/CLAUDE.md`, the paragraph on the shortcode's two value readers; do not re-flag without a real incident.
- **A condition added to a probe is not landed until it is measured red under a mutation of the thing it watches.** The whole subject of the 2026-10-29 pass was conditions that could not fail: a caption comparison truncated at the first non-breaking space, callout bodies nobody read, a template payload no condition reached, and a probe shape whose removal nothing saw. Every condition that pass added was checked red on a copy of the repository under the scratchpad, never on the tracked tree, and one of them was wrong on first write: `t.find(...) or {}` can never match, an ElementTree element with no children being falsy.
- **`depth=0`/negative clamping to `depth=1` is coherent, not a silent-coercion bug.** `scan`'s `depth > 1` guard sends 0 and 1 down the same `has_visible_entry` branch; the render does not lie. Rejected. Note: the sibling coercions raised by the same finding (`depth="abc"`, non-canonical `hidden` spellings, unknown kwargs, positional args) WERE worth cheap `warn()`s and were fixed, on the "a call site that misses the documented form should be named, not absorbed" principle.
