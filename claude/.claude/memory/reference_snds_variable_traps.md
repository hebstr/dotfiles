Verified 2026-10-02 on the official SNDS documentation, while declaring the extraction of the national arm of eds-prise, whose project memory lives in that repository's own `.claude/memory/`. Each item is a place where the obvious variable is the wrong one.

- **An ALD is selected by its diagnosis, never by its number.** `IR_IMB_R` carries `IMB_ALD_NUM`, which the documentation calls poorly populated and which is not corrected when the regulation changes. The recommended route rebuilds the ALD code from `MED_MTF_COD`, declared `length: 6` and so carrying the full ICD-10 code rather than its three-character category, joined to the `IR_CIM_V` referential on `CIM_COD`, which carries `ALD_030_COD`; a three-character declared pattern is therefore a prefix match, not an equality. **The schema wires `MED_MTF_COD` to `IR_CCI_V` instead**, the three-character category table over 2 066 rows, which a six-character code cannot join without truncation, so the fiche's pointer beats the schema's here, and `IMB_ALD_NUM`'s own `observation` names `IR_CIM_V` and `IR_ALD_V` as the referentials to search. The distinction the join buys is an ALD of the list of 30 against any exoneration carrying that diagnosis: the 57 codes of `E1[0-4]` split 23 on ALD 8, 32 on ALD 99 and 2 on ALD 17. Companion variables: `IMB_ETM_NAT` (41 list, 43 off-list, 45 polypathology) and the dates `IMB_ALD_DTD` / `IMB_ALD_DTF`. So a declaration lists ICD-10 codes, not ALD numbers.
- **A CIP list is derived from an ATC class, never typed.** `IR_PHA_R` maps CIP to ATC through `PHA_ATC_CLA`, the seven-character code, beside `PHA_ATC_C03` on three characters and the labels `PHA_ATC_L03` and `PHA_ATC_LIB`; `ER_PHA_F` carries `PHA_PRS_C13` (CIP13) and `PHA_PRS_IDE` (CIP7), and the join runs on CIP13, `IR_PHA_R`'s primary key. `ER_PHA_F` holds no date at all, so `EXE_SOI_DTD` comes from `ER_PRS_F` through its nine-key foreign key. **The *Médicament* fiche still writes `PHA_ATC_C07`** in both its reference queries, with the same `substr(...,1,5)` semantics: it is the field's former name, absent from all 827 schemas at tag `6.0.0`, so the pinned schema decides over the prose fiche. ATC does not express the galenic form but the referential does, and **the field is `PHA_FRC_COD`, never the label `PHA_FRC_LIB`**: the label is empty on 14 of the 138 `H02AB` presentations, truncated at 40 characters and carries spelling variants, so `str_detect(PHA_FRC_LIB, "INJECT")` returns 28 presentations where `PHA_FRC_COD` in (281, 295, 314, 522) returns 31, losing `DEPO-MEDROL 40 MG/1 ML` among others and gaining a nasal form under code 21. Neither yields the injectable set mechanically, code 566 holding injectables ambiguously, so the referential narrows a published speciality list without replacing it, the periarticular marketing authorisation being a subset `IR_PHA_R` carries no indication for. **The site publishes no page for a nomenclature**: `/tables/ir_pha_r/`, `/tables/ir_cim_v/` and `/tables/ir_spe_v/` all answer 404 where table schemas answer 200, so a nomenclature is documented by its pinned JSON, its CSV and a thematic fiche alone.
- **`BEN_CMU_TOP` of `ER_PRS_F` is forced to 0 when the benefit is liquidated under an ALD**, so it under-counts exactly in a comorbid population, and it marks an exonerated prestation rather than a person. The entitlement is `BEN_CTA_TYP` = 89, stable across the 2019-11-01 CMU-C to C2S switch, and **it sits on two tables whose limits are opposite**: on `IR_ORC_R` it is exhaustive over consumers and non-consumers alike, which both fiches recommend for a population count, but that table covers « uniquement le champ du Régime Général et des SLM », so a non-RG beneficiary has no row and reads as uncovered rather than as missing; on `ER_PRS_F` it covers every régime and dates itself by the prestation, at the price of seeing consumers only, systematically populated from January 2016 after a 2015 ramp-up. A cohort defined by an act is therefore better served by `ER_PRS_F`, a prevalence study by `IR_ORC_R`. `IR_ORC_R` is an interval history read through `MLL_CTA_DSD` and `MLL_CTA_DSF`, whose null means an open contract; `REF_C2S_COD` separates C2S gratuite from participative but only from 2021, and `IR_ACS_R` complements neither, carrying rights locking and no entitlement. **One validity limit precedes the choice of table**: the *CMU Complémentaire* fiche declares the indicator irrelevant as a precarity measure above 60, the low-income elderly drawing the ASPA, whose amount exceeds the eligibility ceiling.
- **Four reading traps of `IR_IMB_R` that no field name announces.** `IMB_ETM_NAT` = 45, polypathology, « remonte seulement pour la MSA à partir de 2014 », the régime général being fed « à l'exception des polypathologies », so ALD 32 is effectively unavailable nationally and a diagnosis absent from the list of 30 is reachable only as an ALD 31 under motive 43: `IR_CIM_V` gives all six `E66*` codes an `ALD_030_COD` of 99, which is what makes obesity uncountable this way. The table is **not deduplicated**, a renewal or a correction inserting a new row rather than overwriting, so the selection runs on `INS_DTE`, and `UPD_DTE` is a false friend, incremented when nothing changed and missing when a modification occurred. A granted ALD is not an active one: 16 % of the referential's beneficiaries had no exonerated prestation over 2017 and 5 % of exonerated patients were absent from the referential, the CNAM advising a crossing with `EXO_MTF` of `ER_PRS_F`. And `MED_NCL_IDT` must be filtered to `"CIM10"`, `IR_NMD_V` also carrying `CIM9` and `ISO3950`.
- **AME beneficiaries cannot be counted as people, only as activity**: their NIR is often provisional, so longitudinal follow-up fails. Identified by `RGM_COD` in (95, 96, 652) or `BEN_CMU_CAT` = 5 in `ER_PRS_F`; not applicable in Mayotte.
- **The deprivation index is in the base**, tables `DEFA_UU2009` / `DEFA_UU2013` / `DEFA_UU2015` (library `CONSOPAT`), raw index `FDEPaa`, quintiles `QUINTILE_COM` and `QUINTILE_POP`, joined by `depcom`, the concatenation of `BEN_RES_DPT` and `BEN_RES_COM` of `IR_BEN_R`. A precomputed `QUINT_DEFA` exists in `EXTRACTION_PATIENTSaaaa`. It characterises the commune, not the patient, and the documentation warns against it on large heterogeneous communes.
- **2015 is the depth that matters, not the first year present.** The documentation states that 2015 is the year from which all social security regimes report sufficiently exhaustively; the DCIR goes back to 2006 for the régime général alone, and `IR_IMB_R` starts 2005 for the RG, 2014 for the MSA and 2016 for the RSI. Years before 2015 are usable in exclusion-only lookback, where incompleteness never includes wrongly.
- **NGAP acts have no thematic fiche, but the nomenclature settles them.** The route for physiotherapy was taken from a 2019 community forum answer and treated as unconfirmed until `IR_NAT_V`, the nomenclature of `PRS_NAT_REF` on `ER_PRS_F`, was read at tag `6.0.0`: it carries `3125 AMS ACTES DE KINESITHERAPIE OSTEO-ARTICULAIRE`, `3122 AMK ACTES EN AMK` and `3121 AMC ACTES AMC`. So the filter runs on the numeric `PRS_NAT_REF`, the letters being the nomenclature's own `PRS_NAT_CB2`. **The same nomenclature is why `ER_PRS_F` cannot target a CCAM act**: it stops at the family (`ADC` chirurgie, `ADE` échographie, `ADI` imagerie hors échographie, `ATM` actes techniques médicaux), so the code itself is `CAM_PRS_IDE` on `ER_CAM_F`, joined on nine keys. **`IR_NAT_V` labels `3121 AMC` only « ACTES AMC »; `IR_ACT_COD`, the DREES nomenclature of NGAP key letters, defines it**: `AMC` « actes de kinésithérapie en établissement », `AMK` « actes de kinésithérapie », `AMS` « suivi des actes de kinésithérapie ». `IR_ACT_COD` is also the nomenclature of `ACT_COD` on the ACE tables (`T_MCOaaFBSTC`), so the same letters read a hospital outpatient session.
- **The DCIR is « soins de ville » by label only: it holds public-hospital ACE that the PMSI also holds, verified 2026-10-03.** The glossary says the DCIR contains « l'ensemble des soins de ville », but the CNAM communiqué of 2026-07-08 (`snds/CNAM/dcir_et_referentiels/dcir/08.DCIR-Exclusion_ACE_Macro-Com08072026`, PDF alongside) and slides 15 to 17, 137 to 141 and 219 of its DCIR course (`16_NEW_Module_Socle-3_DONNEES_DCIR_Cours_V7-01Juillet2026.pptx`, text only in the `.pptx`, the `.md` being a stub) show it carries public-establishment ACE, FIDES-billed or transmitted for information, while `T_MCOaaFASTC` and `T_MCOaaFMSTC` hold them all. Counting an outpatient act from both sources counts it twice. The filter joins `ER_ETE_F` to `ER_PRS_F` and reads `DPN_QLF` (`<> 71`, and `<> 72` in the slides, the communiqué naming 71 only) with `ETE_IND_TAA`, `PRS_PPU_SEC` and `ETE_CAT_COD in (101,355,131,106,122,365,128,129)`: `ETE_IND_TAA` = 1 alone is not enough, since some public MCO flows, AME care among them, sit at 0. These three variables exist on `ER_ETE_F` only, so a declaration that omits that table cannot apply the filter. **Private-establishment stays are the other half**: the acts of a private stay are in both `ER_CAM_F` and `T_MCOaaA` (`STA_ETA = 'OQN'`), public stays in the PMSI alone (`'DGF'`), and `STA_ETA` is on `T_MCOaaE` only. The CNAM reference query (`09.HELP_SNDS_seance7_actes.pdf`, Help SNDS 2025-09-18) reads the DCIR for town and private clinics, `T_MCOaaFMSTC` for public ACE and `T_MCOaaA` with `STA_ETA = 'DGF'` for public stays, and also filters `ACV_ACT = '1'` (act priced by the physician) and, in its mammography example, `PHA_ACT = '0'` (no phase; a few acts, tendon surgery among them, have several, so `= '0'` is not a general rule), the schema creating `ACT_ORD_NUM` on `T_MCOaaA` only in 2019.
