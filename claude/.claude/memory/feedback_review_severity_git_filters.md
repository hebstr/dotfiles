On the git content filters of `~/dotfiles/git/.config/git/`, do not file a finding whose load-bearing claim is what Word, PowerPoint or Excel does when a human saves the file. Nothing installed here settles it: `rules/docx.md` closes that question outright under "What nothing on this machine settles", and the three substitute routes are measured and rejected in `~/dotfiles/_meta/notes/docx-verification.md`. Such an observation belongs in the decision note that owns the filter, as a question a licensed Word must answer, never as a finding with a remedy attached. The pass through the real Word ran on 2026-10-25 and settles the render half alone: that Word is unlicensed, so it lays out and paginates but writes no file, and no save can be measured on ju-TP2 at all.

Check two things before filing anything about a package member a filter leaves alone:

- whether the member kind occurs in the files the `.gitattributes` pattern reaches, which is narrower than the files that exist. Measured 2026-10-24: no project declares `filter=out-meta` yet, that line sitting in `~/dotfiles/_meta/profiles/gitattributes` alone, and of the 28 tracked OOXML the pattern would reach across the 51 repositories under `~/Documents`, none carries `docProps/thumbnail.jpeg` or a `ppt/printerSettings/` member, while the two tracked `.pptx` that do carry a thumbnail sit under `docs/slides/`, which the pattern does not match;
- whether the author already excluded it by design. For `clean-out-meta.py` the exclusions are the `w:fontKey` GUID, the ODTTF obfuscation header and every PNG pixel payload, and the `metadata-only` prek hook is the declared backstop for all three.

**Why:** the walkthrough of 2026-10-24 rejected one such finding (`docProps/thumbnail.jpeg` and `printerSettings1.bin` pass through unnormalised, "both regenerated by PowerPoint on save"). Both halves failed: the pass-through half is a tautology from the suffix dispatch, and the regeneration half was unsourced, with cross-model L1 and L2 both returning invalid on it. A second finding of the same shape, on `cp:revision` and `cp:lastModifiedBy`, was deferred rather than rejected, because there the pin is two lines and the question is genuinely open; it sits in `~/dotfiles/.claude/DEFERRED.md` waiting on a licensed Word.

**How to apply:** route the observation to the note's "What a licensed Word still owes" section and say so in the review, rather than proposing a tuple entry or a new member handler. This does not relax the excluded categories: a finding of corruption, of silent data loss or of a security defect in these filters stays fully in scope whatever the corpus says, which is how the same pass accepted the `*.png` suffix dispatch replacing the first eight bytes of a non-PNG member, on zero corpus occurrences.
