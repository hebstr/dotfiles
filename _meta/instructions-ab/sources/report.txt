Interim report: outpatient follow-up study

Background
The study follows adults discharged after a first hospitalisation for heart failure and measures readmission within 90 days.

Methods
Recruitment ran in four hospitals between January and June. The target sample size was 420 patients, computed for 80 percent power to detect a 10 point difference in 90-day readmission at a two-sided alpha of 0.05, allowing for 15 percent loss to follow-up.

Results
By the data cut-off, 377 patients had been enrolled, 90 percent of the target. Follow-up is complete for 312 of them.

Next steps
Recruitment is extended by two months to reach the planned sample size.
